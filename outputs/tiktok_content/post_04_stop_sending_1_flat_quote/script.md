## 2. Full Teleprompter Script (Word-for-Word)

[LEAN IN - DIRECT EYE CONTACT]
If your technicians are quoting homeowners one single price for an equipment replacement, you are literally giving away 22% of your profit margin every single day.

[HOLD UP 1 FINGER]
When you give a customer one number—say $4,200—the only decision in their brain is: 'Yes or No? Do I pay this guy or call someone else?'

[HOLD UP 3 FINGERS]
When you give them THREE choices—Good, Better, Best—their psychology flips from 'Do I hire this company?' to 'Which tier do I want?'

[SMILE]
Over 60% of homeowners choose the middle option, and 15% choose the highest tier with premium warranties. Stop quoting flat numbers.
