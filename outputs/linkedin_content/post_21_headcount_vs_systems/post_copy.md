Stop hiring more dispatchers to fix missed calls.

When trade contractors experience customer support bottlenecks, the default reaction is: "We need to hire another CSR or night dispatcher."

That adds $45,000 to $60,000 in annual payroll, plus payroll taxes, training, and management overhead.

Yet three months later, response times are still sluggish.

Why?

Because 70% of customer inquiries are repetitive, low-complexity tasks:
• "When will the technician arrive?"
• "How do I shut off the main water valve?"
• "Can I get a copy of my invoice?"

Automating routine inquiries with real-time GPS tracking SMS and self-serve invoice portals frees up your existing CSRs to focus on high-ticket commercial dispatch.

Scale systems before you scale headcount.

#OperationsLeadership #HiringMistakes #BusinessEfficiency #Automation
