More software won't fix a broken dispatch workflow.

Contractors love buying software. They have ServiceTitan, Jobber, Housecall Pro, Zapier, HubSpot, Mailchimp, and three different messaging widgets.

Yet their office staff still spends 15 hours a week re-typing customer addresses, copy-pasting dispatch notes, and manually texting technicians.

Why?

Because software is not a workflow.

A tool is just a database. A workflow is the automated bridge connecting:
• When Job Signs Off -> Trigger Review SMS
• When Invoice Exceeds $3,000 -> Trigger Warranty Follow-up
• When Single-Trade Customer Is Tagged -> Schedule Cross-Sell Offer

Before you buy another $300/month software subscription, map out the handoffs between the tools you already own.

#SaaS #OperationsArchitecture #WorkflowAutomation #FieldService
