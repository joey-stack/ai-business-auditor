Why your $12,000 equipment quotes land in spam.

You send a detailed commercial proposal or invoice from your CRM or Microsoft 365 / Google Workspace. Three days later, the client says: "I never got it."

When we audit contractor IT infrastructure, 7 out of 10 times we find an unauthenticated legacy SPF record or missing DMARC enforcement.

If your domain's SPF record has a neutral "?all" mechanism or omits your active CRM sending IP, modern inbox filters (Gmail, Outlook) automatically quarantine your emails.

The financial fallout:
• Delayed payment cycles
• Lost commercial proposals
• Customer payment disputes

Fixing your DNS records (SPF, DKIM, DMARC p=quarantine) takes less than 120 minutes and restores 99.8%+ inbox deliverability.

Don't let DNS misconfigurations silently choke your cash flow.
