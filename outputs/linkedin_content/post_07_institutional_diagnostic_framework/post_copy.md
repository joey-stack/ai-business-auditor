Inside a 4-Pillar Systems Audit for a $5M fleet.

Most business audits are full of vague consulting buzzwords: "Improve synergy," "Adopt AI," "Streamline processes."

When we audit a $1M–$10M service enterprise, we use a rigid 4-step reasoning scaffold:
1. Evidence: Observed telemetry from DNS records, server response latency, review velocity, and CRM intake routes.
2. Expected State: The operational benchmark for a top-decile enterprise of this scale.
3. Gap Magnitude: The measured distance between reality and benchmark.
4. Dollarized Leakage: The exact monthly and annual revenue forfeited across each bottleneck.

In our latest diagnostic of a 15-van contractor, this model uncovered $822,000 in annualized revenue leakage ($68,500/month).

Curious what this deliverable looks like?

I've uploaded the complete 4-page anonymized sample audit right to my Featured section above. Feel free to review it and compare it against your own operations.
