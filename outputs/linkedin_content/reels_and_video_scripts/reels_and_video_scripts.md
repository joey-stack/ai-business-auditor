## Video Script #1: The 60-Second Lead Leakage Test

* **Visual Hook**: Walking shot in office or looking directly into camera with phone in hand.
* **On-Screen Text Overlay**: "The 60-second test that costs contractors $14,500/month."
* **Duration**: 50 seconds

### Teleprompter Script:
> "(Point at camera) If you run a plumbing or HVAC business, I want you to do a test tonight at 8:45 PM.  
> 
> Have someone call your main office phone number from a personal cell.  
> 
> Does an active dispatcher pick up? Does an automated text immediately reply with water shutoff safety instructions?  
> 
> Or does it ring 5 times and hit a voicemail that says: 'Leave a message and we'll call you back at 8:00 AM'?  
> 
> Here's what most owners don't realize: when a homeowner has a burst pipe or broken AC in July, they don't leave voicemails. They hang up within 6 seconds and call your competitor.  
> 
> In our recent audits of 15-van fleets, this single leak costs businesses $14,500 every single month in abandoned jobs.  
> 
> You don't need to hire night dispatchers. You need an automated emergency triage flow that books arrival windows in under 60 seconds.  
> 
> If you want to see the exact numbers behind this, I pinned a full 4-page diagnostic in my LinkedIn Featured section. Go take a look."

## Video Script #2: Why Your $15,000 Commercial Quotes Land in Spam

* **Visual Hook**: Showing laptop screen with an email inbox or standing in front of a whiteboard.
* **On-Screen Text Overlay**: "Why clients say 'I never got your quote' (And how to fix it in 2 hours)."
* **Duration**: 55 seconds

### Teleprompter Script:
> "Ever send a $12,000 or $15,000 replacement proposal, and 4 days later the customer tells you: 'I never got it'?  
> 
> Most business owners blame the customer. But 7 times out of 10, it's actually your DNS records.  
> 
> When you send an estimate from your CRM or Microsoft 365, Google and Outlook check whether your domain has authorized that specific server.  
> 
> If your SPF TXT record still has a legacy hosting provider like Bluehost or GoDaddy from 6 years ago, or has a soft '?all' mechanism...  
> 
> Gmail and Outlook silently dump your estimate straight into spam or quarantine.  
> 
> That means:
> • Delayed approvals  
> • Cash flow bottlenecks  
> • Lost high-margin equipment jobs  
> 
> Fixing this takes less than 2 hours: align your SPF, enable DKIM signing, and set up DMARC.  
> 
> Stop letting broken DNS settings kill your close rate. Check my Featured section for the technical roadmap."

## Video Script #3: The Good / Better / Best Quoting Hack (+22% Lift)

* **Visual Hook**: Holding a sheet of paper or showing a field tablet with 3 pricing columns.
* **On-Screen Text Overlay**: "How choice architecture adds 22% margin to equipment replacements."
* **Duration**: 45 seconds

### Teleprompter Script:
> "If your technicians are quoting water heaters or heat pump replacements as a single flat number, you are accidentally leaving 20% to 25% of your profit on the kitchen table.  
> 
> Here's the psychology:  
> When you show a homeowner one price—say $5,500—their brain asks a binary question:  
> 'Should I buy this, or should I get a second quote from someone else?'  
> 
> But when you present a 3-tier Good / Better / Best proposal:  
> • Tier 1: Standard replacement  
> • Tier 2: Upgraded efficiency + 5-year warranty  
> • Tier 3: Premium unit + whole-home filtration + annual membership  
> 
> Their brain completely shifts. Now they ask:  
> 'Which of these three is the best fit for my home?'  
> 
> Over 60% of homeowners choose Tier 2 or Tier 3. That’s a 22% average ticket lift without spending a dime on marketing.  
> 
> Choice architecture works. Stop quoting single prices."
