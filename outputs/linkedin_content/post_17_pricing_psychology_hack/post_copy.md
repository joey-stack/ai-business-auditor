Stop sending flat quotes. Give customers 3 choices.

When you show a homeowner one price—say $5,500—their brain asks a binary question: "Should I buy this, or should I get a second quote from someone else?"

But when you present a 3-tier Good / Better / Best proposal:
• Tier 1: Standard replacement
• Tier 2: Upgraded efficiency + 5-year warranty
• Tier 3: Premium unit + whole-home filtration + annual membership

Their brain completely shifts. Now they ask:
"Which of these three is the best fit for my home?"

Over 60% of homeowners choose Tier 2 or Tier 3. That’s a 22% average ticket lift without spending a dime on marketing.

Choice architecture works. Stop quoting single prices.
