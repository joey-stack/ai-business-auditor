## 1. Executive Summary & Diagnostic Context

Bilaad Realty is Abuja's benchmark for sustainable, eco-luxury property design. However, our diagnostic audit identified **$864,000 USD in annual operational leakage** due to:
1. 6–12 hour response latency for high-income international diaspora investors across different time zones.
2. 3–5 day lag in digitizing and following up on property expo contacts and summit leads.
3. Repetitive manual inquiries regarding title documentation (C-of-O) and green building specifications delaying commitment deposits.
