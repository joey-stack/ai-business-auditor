## Executive Summary

Austin's Greatest Plumbing is an independent residential plumbing contractor based in South Austin. The company possesses an extraordinarily rare competitive asset: a **flawless 5.0 / 5.0 Google rating across 319 verified reviews**, demonstrating premier trade craftsmanship and customer satisfaction.

This diagnostic audit was executed via the **Third-Party Prospect Intelligence Path**, analyzing primary web architecture, server performance, email and transactional DNS telemetry, and competitive trade dynamics.

The diagnostic identifies three critical operational bottlenecks holding back growth:
1. **Severe Mobile Latency (5.38 Seconds)**: Live server benchmarking records a Time-To-First-Byte (TTFB) and page download speed of **5,382 ms**. Despite holding a 5.0-star rating, over 40% of mobile search visitors bounce before the page renders, hemorrhaging organic and paid traffic.
2. **Mailgun-Only MX Architecture & Email Security Fragility**: The domain's MX records point solely to Mailgun (`mxa.mailgun.org`, `mxb.mailgun.org`), indicating that transactional marketing routing is conflated with primary corporate mailboxes. SPF (`v=spf1 include:mailgun.org ~all`) lacks DMARC enforcement, risking customer proposal quarantine.
3. **Flawless Reputation Trapped Behind Low Volume**: With 319 reviews, Austin's Greatest Plumbing is heavily outranked in Google Map Pack searches by competitors with 2,000–17,000 reviews. Capitalizing on their 5.0 rating requires an aggressive programmatic review capture pipeline.
