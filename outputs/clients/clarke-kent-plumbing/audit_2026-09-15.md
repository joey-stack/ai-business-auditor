## Executive Summary

Clarke Kent Plumbing is a long-standing, high-visibility residential plumbing contractor located on West Ben White Blvd in South Austin (78704). Operating in one of Austin's most competitive service corridors, the company holds **611 Google reviews with a 4.5 / 5.0 rating**, reflecting strong local brand recognition alongside noticeable customer dispute friction.

This diagnostic audit was executed via the **Third-Party Prospect Intelligence Path**, analyzing website security, server performance, email infrastructure, and competitive market positioning.

The diagnostic reveals three high-severity operational bottlenecks:
1. **Aggressive Bot Protection Challenge Wall ("One moment, please...")**: Live HTTP benchmarking reveals that the company's web server intercepts standard visitors and automated requests with an intermediate security challenge wall. This blocks legitimate mobile users with strict browser privacy settings and impedes search engine crawler indexing.
2. **Reputation Vulnerability (4.5 Star Rating Drag)**: In a market where competitors average 4.8 to 5.0 stars, a 4.5-star score across 600+ reviews creates consumer hesitation for major ticket repairs (sewer line replacements, whole-home repiping).
3. **Enterprise Email Gateway on Shared cPanel Hosting**: DNS analysis reveals that email is filtered through **Sophos Email Security** (`prod.hydra.sophos.com`) while SPF records authorize legacy shared cPanel servers (`spf.supercp.com`), creating operational friction in administrative communication.

## 90-Day Tactical Quick Wins (< 30 Days to Implement)

1. **Reputation Filter & Private Feedback Loop**: Deploy an automated SMS post-service survey that routes 5-star ratings to Google while directing feedback <4 stars to management for immediate resolution.

3. **Deploy AI Emergency Phone Answering**: Implement an AI call assistant to handle after-hours overflow and quote dispatch arrival windows.
