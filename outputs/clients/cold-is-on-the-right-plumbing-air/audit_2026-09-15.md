## Executive Summary

Cold Is On The Right Plumbing & Air is a dual-trade plumbing and HVAC service contractor located along the FM 620 corridor in Austin, Texas (serving Lake Travis, Lakeway, Bee Cave, and Greater Austin). With a verified **4.8 / 5.0 rating across 292 Google reviews**, the business commands high localized customer satisfaction in affluent western Austin suburbs.

This diagnostic audit was executed via the **Third-Party Prospect Intelligence Path**, analyzing primary web architecture (Divi Builder on WordPress), Google Workspace email infrastructure, DNS records, and competitive trade positioning against market giants (*Radiant Plumbing*, *Reliant Plumbing*, and *Fox Service Company*).

The diagnostic reveals three high-severity leverage points:
1. **Uncaptured HVAC/Plumbing Cross-Sell Velocity**: While operating as a dual-trade provider (plumbing and air conditioning), the company's digital funnel treats the services as independent silos without automated membership agreements or multi-system seasonal maintenance packaging.
2. **After-Hours Emergency Triage Gap**: In the Lakeway/Lake Travis corridor, freeze events, high water pressure bursts, and summer AC outages demand immediate 24/7 responsiveness. The current digital touchpoints lack conversational AI triage, causing high-value off-hours leads to call larger competitors.
3. **Outdated Email SPF/DNS Configuration**: DNS inspection reveals Google Workspace MX (`aspmx.l.google.com`) coupled with a legacy Bluehost SPF record (`v=spf1 a mx ptr include:bluehost.com ?all`) with a neutral `?all` fallback, exposing customer communications to spoofing and deliverability degradation.
