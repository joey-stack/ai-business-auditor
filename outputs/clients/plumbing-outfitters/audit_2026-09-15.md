## Executive Summary

Plumbing Outfitters is a master-plumber-owned contracting firm headquartered on North Mopac in Austin, Texas, with secondary service hubs extending east into Taylor and Williamson County. Holding a **4.9 / 5.0 Google rating across 417 reviews**, the company demonstrates mature operational sophistication, utilizing **ServiceTitan** for field service management alongside **Chiirp** for automated SMS client communications.

This diagnostic audit was executed via the **Third-Party Prospect Intelligence Path**, analyzing primary web architecture, CRM/automation integrations, Google Workspace email routing, and Austin trade competition.

The diagnostic reveals three high-impact growth opportunities:
1. **Underutilized ServiceTitan Automation Capabilities**: While Plumbing Outfitters has invested in the premier field service platform (ServiceTitan), its web booking intake is not fully synced with live online scheduling, leading to dispatch triage bottlenecks.
2. **Suburban Expansion & Local SEO Capture**: Although the firm explicitly highlights services in Taylor and North Austin, organic search dominance in fast-growing Williamson County suburbs (Hutto, Round Rock, Taylor) is currently fragmented.
3. **Complex Multi-Sender SPF Architecture**: The domain's SPF record combines SparkPost, Mailgun, Chiirp SMS, and Google Workspace. Without strict DMARC enforcement (`p=none` or missing policy), automated service reminders risk being filtered by major email providers.
