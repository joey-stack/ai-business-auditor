# Outreach Compliance Standards

All prospect outreach across the AI Business Auditor project must strictly adhere to legal and regulatory communication standards across jurisdictions.

---

## 1. US (CAN-SPAM Act)

- **No Prior Consent Required**: B2B commercial email does not require opt-in or prior consent under federal US law.
- **Mandatory Email Elements**: Every email must include:
  - Accurate and non-deceptive header information (`From`, `Reply-To`, routing info).
  - A non-deceptive, transparent subject line.
  - Clear and conspicuous identification that the message is an advertisement or solicitation.
  - A valid physical postal address of the sender (or registered P.O. Box).
  - A clear, conspicuous, and functioning unsubscribe / opt-out mechanism.
- **Opt-Out Processing Time**: Must honor unsubscribe requests within **10 business days** of receipt.
- **Strict Penalties**: Non-compliance carries penalties of up to **$51,744 per violating email**.

---

## 2. EU & UK (GDPR / PECR)

- **Legitimate Interest Basis**: B2B cold email outreach to corporate entities is permissible under the GDPR Article 6(1)(f) "legitimate interest" legal basis.
- **Role Relevance**: Outreach must be directly relevant to the recipient's professional function or business context.
- **Mandatory Transparency**: Senders must clearly state who they are, the business they represent, the specific reason for reaching out, and the source where contact data was obtained.
- **Immediate Opt-Out**: Senders must provide an effortless opt-out mechanism and honor suppression requests **immediately** upon receipt.
- **Legitimate Interest Assessment (LIA)**: Always document the legitimate interest assessment (balancing business relevance, data minimization, and recipient privacy rights) prior to sending.
- **Sole Trader & Partnership Protection**: In many EU/UK jurisdictions, sole proprietorships and partnerships are treated as individual natural persons (personal data). Cold outreach via personal email addresses requires extreme caution; prefer listed corporate business phone lines or in-person channels.

---

## 3. Phone Outreach (TCPA / TSR / National DNC)

- **Business Line Exemption**: Cold B2B telephone calls placed to clearly designated business lines are generally exempt from the National Do Not Call (DNC) Registry.
- **No Autodialers / Prerecorded Calls**: Strictly avoid automated telephone dialing systems (ATDS), artificial voices, and prerecorded messages without prior express written consent.
- **Immediate Identification**: The caller must immediately state their identity, the business they represent, and the commercial purpose of the call.
- **Honor Do-Not-Call Requests**: Immediately cease calls and add the number to an internal suppression list if the recipient requests not to be contacted again.
- **Mobile Number Restrictions**: Cell phones and mobile numbers carry heightened regulatory restrictions and liability under the TCPA. Always prioritize calling the verified, publicly listed primary business landline.
