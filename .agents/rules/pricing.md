# Pricing Tiers & Service Packages

This document defines the standard engagement tiers for AI and digital optimization services. All proposals generated by `proposal-generator` must draw pricing and deliverables strictly from these defined tiers.

---

## 1. Starter Tier — $750 / month (or $2,000 one-off setup)
**Ideal for**: Local single-location trade businesses or solo operators needing foundational digital presence and local discoverability.

### Deliverables:
- **Google Business Profile (GBP) Full Optimization**: Category refinement, photo updates, primary keyword placement, geotag alignment.
- **NAP Consistency & Directory Citations**: Synchronization across top 25 local citation directories (Yelp, YellowPages, Bing Places, Apple Maps).
- **Automated Post-Service Review Collection**: QR code and SMS-based review capture system to systematically route satisfied clients to Google Reviews.
- **Monthly Local Health Report**: Monthly rank tracking and review volume audit.

---

## 2. Standard Tier — $1,750 / month
**Ideal for**: Established contractors and professional firms (5–20 staff) seeking consistent inbound inquiry flow and modern web conversions.

### Deliverables:
- **Everything in Starter**, plus:
- **High-Converting Fast Landing Page / Web Redesign**: Sub-1.5s Core Web Vitals, mobile-first design, automated appointment scheduling widget.
- **10 Strategic Local Target Keywords**: On-page SEO optimization and local intent landing pages.
- **Monthly Content Operations**: 2 localized case studies or educational articles driving local search authority.
- **Active Review Management**: Automated AI-assisted review response drafting within 24 hours.
- **Bi-Weekly Consultation**: 30-minute operational review with Joel Adawah Sani.

---

## 3. Premium Tier — $3,500 / month
**Ideal for**: High-volume regional operators (20–50+ staff) requiring comprehensive omnichannel automation, lead qualification, and continuous competitive displacement.

### Deliverables:
- **Everything in Standard**, plus:
- **Custom AI Receptionist / Lead Triage Bot**: 24/7 automated inquiry capture, quote pre-qualification, and calendar booking integrated with CRM.
- **Full Regional Organic & Technical SEO**: Dynamic schema markup, deep backlink acquisition, multi-location service area pages.
- **Continuous Competitor Monitoring**: Monthly tracking against top 3 local competitors with proactive gap exploitation.
- **Automated Operations Integration**: Zapier/Make workflow automation connecting web inquiries directly to dispatch/field management software (ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro, Jobber).
- **Executive Monthly Deep Dive**: Detailed ROI reporting, pipeline metrics, and priority technical support.
